Questions people ask

Where is The Genesis Hub located?

Beside NIPCO Gas Station, A&T Hotel Close, off Oloko Junction, Akure, Ondo State. It is near FUTA.

What does The Genesis Hub offer?

Hot desks and daily coworking, dedicated and private offices, a conference hall for corporate events, and the Startup Launchpad incubation and acceleration programme.

Does The Genesis Hub have stable power and internet?

Yes. The Hub provides high-speed internet and 24/7 power, with security on site.

Can I rent the hall for a tech meetup, hackathon or corporate workshop?

Yes. The conference hall is available for tech meetups, hackathons, corporate workshops and similar events in Akure.

What is The Genesis Company?

The Genesis Company is the innovation ecosystem builder behind The Genesis Hub. It runs acceleration programmes, matches talent with teams, and provides enterprise strategy for SMEs.

How do I book a workspace or venue?

Use the Book a Workspace button on this page to message The Genesis Hub and tell the team whether you need a desk, an office or the event hall.

How much does coworking cost at The Genesis Hub?

Daily access is ₦2,000, weekly access is ₦12,000 and monthly access is ₦40,000.

How much does it cost to rent the event hall?

For corporate events (trainings, workshops, seminars, board meetings), 50 heads costs ₦20,000 per hour or ₦160,000 per day (9am-5pm), and 100 heads costs ₦22,000 per hour or ₦200,000 per day. For social events (parties, cocktails, networking, hangouts, freshers or FYB parties), 50 heads costs ₦27,000 per hour or ₦220,000 per day, and 100 heads costs ₦30,000 per hour or ₦240,000 per day.

What is included in the event hall packages?

Every package includes a PA system with 2 microphones, a digital display screen, 8KVA power supply for 8 hours, 2 restrooms and parking space. An advanced sound and audio setup (mixer amp plus lapel and headset microphones) is available as an add-on for ₦40,000.

Is there a security deposit for the event hall?

Yes. The security deposit is ₦50,000 and is refundable within 48 hours after inspection. Standard cleaning and fuel for the 8KVA generator are included in the price.

What if my event has more than 100 guests?

Capacities above 100 guests are classified as Special Plans and are quoted on a custom basis. Contact The Genesis Hub for a quote.
